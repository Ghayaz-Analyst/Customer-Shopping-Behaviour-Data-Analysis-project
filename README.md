# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
 
End-to-end data analytics project covering SQL, Python, and Power BI — built to answer real business questions from raw customer transaction data and presented live during Real Time Training at KG INFO TECH, Hyderabad.
 
**Live dashboard & full portfolio breakdown:** https://syed-ghayaz-uddin-portfolio.netlify.app
 
---
 
## Overview
 
This project analyzes 3,900 customer transactions to uncover patterns in spending, discounts, product ratings, shipping preferences, and subscription behavior — and turns those findings into an interactive Power BI dashboard and a business-facing presentation.
 
## Dataset
 
- **Rows:** 3,900 customer transactions
- **Columns:** 18 (customer demographics and purchase behavior)
- **Data quality:** 37 missing values, all in the Review Rating column — handled with median imputation
 
## Tools Used
 
| Stage | Tool |
|---|---|
| Data storage & querying | SQL (PostgreSQL) |
| Data cleaning & validation | Python (Pandas, NumPy) in Jupyter Notebook |
| Dashboard & visualization | Power BI (DAX measures) |
| Presentation | PowerPoint |
 
## Workflow / Pipeline
 
1. **Raw Data** — customer transaction CSV, uncleaned
2. **SQL** — cleaning, joins, aggregations, and business queries
3. **Python** — exploration and validation in Jupyter Notebook
4. **Power BI** — interactive Customer Behavior Dashboard
5. **Presentation** — PowerPoint summary and live walkthrough
 
## Business Questions Answered (SQL)
 
- What is the total revenue generated by male vs. female customers?
- Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?
- Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review rating?
- How does the average purchase amount compare between Standard and Express shipping?
- Do subscribed customers spend more than non-subscribers, in both average spend and total revenue?
 
## Dashboard Highlights
 
The Power BI "Customer Behavior Dashboard" includes:
 
- KPI cards: number of customers, average purchase amount, average review rating
- % of customers by subscription status (donut chart)
- Revenue and sales by category
- Revenue and sales by age group
- Filters for gender, category, and shipping type
 
## Key Insights
 
- Female customers generate slightly higher total revenue than male customers
- A meaningful segment of customers use discounts while still spending above average — a strong upsell/targeting opportunity
- Express shipping customers spend ~12% more per transaction than Standard shipping customers
- Subscribers show notably higher spend and repeat purchase frequency than non-subscribers
